# Research: CCP's cancelled *World of Darkness* MMO

This summarises what is publicly known about CCP Games' vampire MMO (2006–2014) and which of its
ideas shaped *Sanguine City*. It was compiled from press coverage and forum reports in November 2026.

## Are there design documents?

**No official design documents were ever released.** CCP never published a GDD, and the project
was cancelled before launch. The available primary material comes down to:

| Material | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Alpha playtest manual (March 2014)** | A confidential CCP document handed to internal playtesters. After the cancellation, a Reddit user (reported as *Idoiocracy*) posted it with in-game screenshots in late April 2014. A forum reader described it as about 33 pages covering the game's systems. | Leaked, unofficial. It was not retrieved for this project (the session's network policy blocked the hosting sites), and it is **not reproduced here**: it is CCP's confidential material. |
| **Leaked alpha screenshots** | Screenshots from the same leak, showing the Atlanta-like city, character creation and the HUD. | Leaked, covered by Engadget, AusGamers, NeoGAF, MMORPG.com. |
| **Official interviews and statements (2006–2013)** | Fan Fest talks and press interviews with CCP staff (e.g. creative director Reynir Hardarson). | Public, but sparse on mechanics. |
| **Cloth and hair simulation tech demo** | A CCP tech showcase built for the game. | Public, but contains no gameplay design. |

## What the sources say about the design

**Confirmed by several press outlets:**

- An **EVE-style sandbox** with **politics as the core**. Reynir Hardarson called it "very much inspired by EVE".
- **Open PvP that is mostly free-for-all**, except in **Elysium**, the neutral ground of the tabletop setting
  (PC Gamer, 2013). Players also had personal **havens** that were safe.
- **The Masquerade had consequences:** acting openly as a vampire, or committing PvP in front of humans, carried penalties.
- The March 2014 build had **four playable clans**, each with its own powers.
- Players could **hunt humans** for blood.
- Players could **send ghouls (minions) on missions** to manipulate human society, then use that influence politically.
- The game also planned some **traditional PvE**, to broaden its appeal.
- **Cancellation:** announced 14 April 2014. CCP said the project had not left the pre-production stage, and about 56 staff at
  CCP Atlanta were laid off.

**Reported by readers of the leaked manual (second-hand, less reliable):**

- Character creation started as a human, before the Embrace.
- Core stats included **Humanity**, **Vitae** (blood) and **Prestige** (social standing).
- The manual described **broods** (sire/childe groups with membership rules), **hunting grounds**, zones and a political system.
- Low Humanity reportedly flagged a vampire for PvP.

**Speculative or low-confidence (AI-generated knowledge sites, fan wikis):**

- A "heat" or threat meter that summons NPC hunters when the Masquerade is breached.
- Reported influences from DayZ and Rust (survival-sandbox PvP).

## From inspiration to original design

*World of Darkness* and *Vampire: The Masquerade* (clans, the Camarilla, the Masquerade, Elysium,
Vitae and so on) are intellectual property now owned by Paradox Interactive. *Sanguine City* takes
**genre conventions and mechanical ideas** from the research and expresses them in an original setting:

| CCP WoD concept (from research) | Sanguine City (original) |
|---|---|
| Masquerade | **The Hush**: an Exposure meter fed by witnesses; hunters at 50%, Marked at 80% |
| Humanity | **Conscience**: lost by killing mortals, regained by restraint; at 3 or less "the Beast" marks you |
| Vitae | **Blood**: fades over time and fuels gifts and healing |
| Prestige | **Standing**: earned by missions, justice and district control; weights election votes |
| Four clans with clan powers | Four original **bloodlines**: Vorlen, Sylphine, Ashborn, Aurum |
| Elysium | **Sanctums**: no violence and no feeding, also sheltered from the sun |
| Haven | Bloodline **havens**: PvP-safe, sun-safe, respawn point |
| Ghoul missions to sway society | **Thralls** on timed missions that win **district influence** for your circle |
| Broods / coteries | **Circles**: founded by players, with invites, a founder and kicks |
| Politics sandbox | Nightly **Regent election** with standing-weighted votes; the Regent issues edicts and one **blood hunt** per night |
| Open PvP with consequences | Open PvP outside safe zones; killing an unmarked vampire makes you an **Outlaw** |
| Atlanta setting | **Sanguine City**, a fictional harbour city in four districts |
